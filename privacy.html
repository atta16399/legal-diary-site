Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Legal Diary handles information related to the use of the app and this website. The app is intended for legal professionals and is designed to help manage court dates, client details, and case notes.

Information you provide

Information such as case names, hearing dates, notes, and client details may be entered by the user to support diary and reminder features. This information is used only to provide the core functionality of the service and is not sold or used for unrelated commercial purposes.

Local storage and reminders

Where applicable, reminders and diary data may be stored locally on the device to support timely alerts and offline access. You can manage notification settings from your device settings at any time.

Advertising and analytics

If the app includes advertising, Google AdMob or similar providers may use advertising identifiers and device-related information to display personalised or contextual advertisements and measure performance. Please review Google’s advertising policy and device settings for more detail on how ad data is used.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers that operate the application or advertising systems, as required to support the service.

Children

Legal Diary is intended for use by legal professionals and is not directed to children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@legaldiary.app.
